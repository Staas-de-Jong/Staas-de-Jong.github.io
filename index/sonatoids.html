Sonatoids

I'm lucky to have been put on piano lessons as a child. Only after two decades, however, did I start to play small classical pieces of my own full choice by heart, when having the objective of conveying personal emotions to concrete people via music rather than words.

A few months into this, something new happened which honestly surprised me: I gradually started to develop a small piece in basic sonata-like form, myself.

It was exciting to now turn to composers of pieces I was practicing as de facto teachers.


It was also quite freeing, as a musical amateur: A world that had seemed hermetically closed, at a late stage, suddenly, turned out to be accessible.
